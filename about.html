Our Story

15+ Years of Crafting Joy for Children

Jiaxing Weeyuan Toys was established in 2010 in Jiaxing, Zhejiang Province — located in china's most prominent kids electric ride on cars manufacturing hubs. The industrial supporting facilities are comprehensive.

From day one, our mission has been clear: to ensure the safety of manufacturing and supply, durable, and exciting electric ride-on toys that bring smiles to children's faces while giving parents peace of mind. Over the years, we have shipped over 1 million units to more than 50 countries across Europe, North America, South America, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer service has made us a preferred partner for toy wholesalers, retail chains, and other clients worldwide.
