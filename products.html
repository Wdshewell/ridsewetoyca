FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about products and services

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +

Our standard MOQ is can be flexible per model for wholesale orders. For OEM/ODM projects, the MOQ may vary from 50-100 pieces depending on customization requirements. Contact us for specific MOQ details.

Products come with safety certifications? +

Yes, all products are tested and certified to meet CE, EN71 (Europe), and ASTM F963 (USA) safety standards. Can provide certification documents upon request with your order.

What is the typical production lead time? +

For standard products, the production lead time is 15-25 days depending on order quantity. For OEM/ODM orders, it typically takes 28-35 days including sample development and approval.

Can request product samples before placing a bulk order? +

Absolutely. We encourage all new clients to request samples. Sample costs are based on the product price, and we can often refund the sample fee when you place a bulk order.

What delivery method will be used for the goods? +

Sea freight (most cost-effective for bulk orders), air freight (faster delivery), and express courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS for samples). We can handle FOB, CFR, CIF, DDU, and other based on your preference.

Offer after-sales support? +

Yes, we provide comprehensive after-sales support including spare parts supply, technical guidance, and warranty service, and ensure your customers are satisfied. Supply is just the beginning.
